7. LIVE AGENT FINDINGS DURING VERIFICATION

7.1 Initial live regressions found

During live agent conversation testing against the real service stack, two release-blocking regressions were found:

- `remove coffee` during checkout returned a generic help-style response instead of mutating the cart
- `show my order` during checkout was misrouted away from order status behavior

7.2 Root cause

Root cause was not route-level breakage.
It was intent/workflow interaction in `app/services/agent/policy.py`:

- checkout workflow bias was overpowering explicit cart mutation and order-status signals
- cart parsing heuristic was too broad and accidentally pulled non-cart text into cart intent

7.3 Fix applied

The fix was applied at the policy layer, not as a hardcoded route special-case:

- explicit cart mutation phrases now retain cart intent even inside checkout
- explicit order-status phrases now properly interrupt checkout
- cart mutation detection was narrowed to explicit mutation language
- address messages are no longer swallowed by cart mutation heuristics

7.4 Post-fix live behavior

Verified live behavior after the fix:
- browse products: passed
- add items to cart: passed
- capture address into preview: passed
- remove item during checkout: passed
- order-status interruption during checkout: passed
